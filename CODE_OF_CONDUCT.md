# Code of conduct

aesx is a small project with a small community. Everyone taking part in it, in issues, pull
requests and security reports, is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith in others;
- keep to the subject, and aim criticism at code and ideas, never at people;
- accept that the maintainer decides what is merged, and that "no" is a complete answer;
- refrain from harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, and publishing anyone's
  private information.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately through the repository's private reporting
channel, <https://github.com/yohasacura/aesx/security/advisories/new>, with "conduct" in the
title. Contributions that break these rules may be edited or removed, and repeat offenders
blocked.
